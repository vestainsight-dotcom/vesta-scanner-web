The names **VESTA** and **VESTA Lab**, and any logos, app icons and other original brand artwork, remain under the control of their rights holder. The Apache License 2.0 does not grant permission to use them; Section 6 of the license states that it "does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor".

Brand artwork currently in this repository:

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `public/demo/icons/icon-192.png` | App icon (bar-chart mark) |
| `public/demo/icons/icon-512.png` | App icon |
| `public/demo/icons/icon-maskable-512.png` | App icon, maskable variant |

These files are included so the local demo works as a complete Progressive Web App. Their presence in the repository does not license them. The icon files are also used as the site icon (favicon).

Screenshots in `docs/images/` show VESTA brand elements (the product name and the interface identity). Those brand elements remain excluded from the Apache License 2.0, even though the screenshots are part of the documentation.

The product name also appears in UI text, the web app manifest, the offline page and page metadata (for example "VESTA Scanner"). Describing where the code came from — "based on VESTA Scanner Web" — is fine.

### 3. Forks and derivative works

You may fork, modify and redistribute the **code** under Apache 2.0. If you do:

- Do not present your fork, deployment or app as an official VESTA product or service, and do not imply endorsement by VESTA Lab.
- Replace the app icons in `public/demo/icons/` with your own artwork before you publish or deploy your fork.
- Change the product name in `lib/i18n/messages.ts`, `public/demo/manifest.webmanifest`, `public/demo/offline.html` and `app/layout.tsx` to a name that cannot be confused with VESTA.
- Keep the demo and simulated-data notices, or make equally clear what kind of data your fork shows.
- Keep `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`, as Apache 2.0 requires.

### 4. Third-party names

Exchange, charting and asset names that appear in the UI or in mock data (for example "Binance", "TradingView", "BTCUSDT") are used only to describe common market conventions. No affiliation with, or endorsement by, those parties is implied. Symbol names in the demo are layout placeholders; every value attached to them is fictional.

### 5. Questions

Raise brand questions by [opening an Issue](https://github.com/vestainsight-dotcom/vesta-scanner-web/issues/new/choose) (Feature request template, title starting with "Brand:"). Do not include personal or confidential information in it.

目前儲存庫中的品牌美術：`public/demo/icons/` 下的四個 PNG 圖示（長條圖標誌）。這些檔案只是為了讓本機展示能以完整 PWA 運作而放入，放在儲存庫中並不代表授權使用；這些圖示也用作網站圖示（favicon）。

`docs/images/` 中的截圖呈現了 VESTA 品牌元素（產品名稱與介面識別）。即使截圖屬於文件的一部分，其中的品牌元素仍不在 Apache License 2.0 授權範圍內。

產品名稱也出現在介面文字、Web App Manifest、離線頁與頁面 metadata（例如「VESTA Scanner」）。標示程式碼來源（例如「based on VESTA Scanner Web」）不受限制。

### 3. Fork 與衍生作品

你可以依 Apache 2.0 fork、修改並再散布**程式碼**。但請：

- 不要把你的 fork、部署或 App 呈現為 VESTA 官方產品或服務，也不要暗示獲得 VESTA Lab 背書。
- 公開發布或部署前，把 `public/demo/icons/` 的圖示換成你自己的美術。
- 把 `lib/i18n/messages.ts`、`public/demo/manifest.webmanifest`、`public/demo/offline.html`、`app/layout.tsx` 裡的產品名稱改成不會與 VESTA 混淆的名稱。
- 保留展示模式與模擬資料的說明，或同樣清楚地標示你的資料性質。
- 依 Apache 2.0 要求保留 `LICENSE` 與 `NOTICE`。

### 4. 第三方名稱

介面或模擬資料中出現的交易所、圖表服務與資產名稱（例如 Binance、TradingView、BTCUSDT）僅用於描述一般市場慣例，不代表與這些單位有任何合作或背書關係。展示中的幣種名稱只是版面示意，所有數值皆為虛構。
